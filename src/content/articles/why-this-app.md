Most workout apps try to be everything for everyone. This one does one thing, a specific, packaged version of rest-pause, and does it completely. Here's what that means in practice.

**It's opinionated by design.** The app doesn't offer you 1,600 exercises and ask what you want to do today. It encodes a method: one working set of three partial sets to failure, 27-second rests, derived rep targets, progression rules, stagnation counting, exercise rotation. Every recommendation has a stated reason. Where a generic tracker records what happened and leaves the thinking to you, this app does the thinking that belongs to the method, because the thinking *is* the method.

**It's laser-focused.** No social feed, no badges, no streaks, no calorie tracking, no AI coach, no annoying ads. Everything in it exists because the method needs it: the timer, the log, the trend, the warm-up circuit. The app would rather you spent 40 minutes training than 40 minutes admiring a dashboard, and the fastest way to make that happen is to not build the dashboard.

**It's fast and light.** A few hundred kilobytes, no bloat, built to work on a five-year-old phone over a weak connection, and fully offline. Screens load instantly because there's almost nothing to load.

**It's free to use.** The full functionality, every exercise, every feature, costs nothing. The straightforward business model: a paid tier for genuine platform value later (reliable locked-screen timers, notifications, sync infrastructure), and if the app has given you value, you can support the project. The training is never held hostage.

**And yes, the gaps in the alternatives are real.** The big trackers' free tiers cap your routines, your custom exercises, your history depth; their complexity serves engagement metrics more than training logic; none of them encode a method's rules, so the best of them can only record. If one of them fits how you train, use it happily. This app isn't competing for the everything-lifter, it's a complete, focused, transparent tool for one proven method, with the philosophy on its sleeve.
